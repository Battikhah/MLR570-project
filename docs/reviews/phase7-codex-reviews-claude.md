## Review outcome

**Score: 4/10. Bottom line: ABANDON the proposal in its current form.** The project question is relevant and the benchmark facts are mostly sound, but the central novelty/ranking is stale: DTO-KD, published at ICLR 2026, directly applies dynamic gradient projection / multi-objective trade-offs to task-versus-distillation KD. The current plan omits it and describes DOT as the optimizer-side state of the art. Further, the report overinterprets a batch gradient cosine as whether a teacher contradicts a label, and the combined 300–620 GPU-hour plan is not justified by the stated estimates or a course-scale semester.

This is not a verdict that PCD-in-KD is uninteresting. A narrower study can test whether PCD's **fixed-priority, secondary-progress constraint** differs from DTO-KD's balanced/Pareto trade-off on an identical loss and benchmark; the answer is not established by prior work. Keep the unreliable-teacher question only as a separately validated, smaller follow-up after defining how corrupted teacher targets are constructed and measured.

In addition, PCD's μ values are geometry-dependent normalized-gradient multipliers, not direct replacements for DKD loss weights α/β. The report correctly calls this a candidate; it should be explicitly exploratory and compared against a normalized weighted CE+TCKD+NCKD control with the same compute and tuning budget.

| CA-AT projection used inside adversarial distillation | **PROCEED WITH CAUTION** for a transplant baseline; **ABANDON** any claim of new projection | CA-AT is a projection-like conflict-aware combination for clean/adversarial training, not KD ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16579)). I found no verified primary-source instance of CA-AT transplanted into ARD/KD. This leaves a possible application experiment, but the mechanism is not novel and a robust-KD proposal requires the transplant baseline. Not a recommended first project because attack and robust-evaluation costs dominate.
| Single-head PCD versus DHO dual-head is a novel comparison | **PROCEED WITH CAUTION** | DHO addresses semi-supervised VLM distillation architecturally; the comparison itself appears open in bounded searches, but this is a setting-specific comparison, not a general optimizer novelty ([DHO arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07675)). **Additional primary-source neighbor:** CORD (arXiv 2610.29965, submitted Oct. 21, 2026) is conflict-aware projection for joint RL and self-distillation, not standard supervised classifier KD; it does not erase the benchmark gap but further narrows generic projection novelty ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29965)).

## 3. Mechanism note reconciliation

The other Codex note exists at `/Users/khalidhamdan/orca/workspaces/AML-Code-and-Paper/research-kd-mech-codex/research/notes/kd-pcd-mechanism-codex.md` and was read-only. **AGREE** with its central algebraic cautions: the K=2 coefficient is relative to normalized gradient geometry; PCD is an adaptive normalized weighted sum in the two-objective case; its secondary progress guarantee does not guarantee primary descent, and optimizer momentum/Adam may alter realized alignment. Near-opposition can make primary margin negative. K≥3 can be infeasible/fallback and requires logging.

**DISAGREE** with the proposal's plain-language interpretation in `IDEA_REPORT.md` I4 / `FINAL_PROPOSAL.md`: “the CE weight ... grows only when the teacher contradicts the label” and the proposed protection interpretation are too strong. What grows is the batch-level CE-gradient coefficient when the normalized batch gradients violate the secondary threshold. A negative inner product does not identify which samples are wrong, and the mechanism does not selectively suppress corrupt examples. The note's C5 explicitly says corruption influences aggregate KD direction and can apply CE globally; retain that caveat in the question, hypotheses, and claims.

## 4. Senior-review critique

### Is I1 + I4 the best project recommendation?

**INTERPRETATION:** I1 is the better course fit and has a feasible bounded first experiment, but the current I1 is too broad. The survey theme is KD plus gradient conflict, so comparing a priority method against DOT **and DTO-KD** on a common CIFAR protocol is a strong fit. The submitted unreliable-teacher proposal gives I4 relevance, but adding it as a second full research question doubles methods/conditions and introduces a different mechanism question. It is not justified as one “modest” study by the estimates. With server access, a small pilot is feasible; the 300-hour minimum-viable plan is still substantial and needs instructor/GPU-owner agreement.

The current rank table gives I1 27 vs I4 26 via ordinal scores without a transparent mapping or uncertainty. Course fit is sound; but novelty scores are overstated after DTO-KD. I4's novelty score and risk score should be reduced, and the ranking should favor I1 narrowly and conditionally.

### Hypotheses H1–H7

- **H1 (K=3 noninferiority):** **UNSUPPORTED as presently specified.** A single τ across P1–P3 is a legitimate hypothesis, but three pairs are not independent replications; define primary outcome, noninferiority margin rationale and multiplicity. Include the normalized scalarization control and report infeasible/fallback frequency. Tuning τ on one pair and freezing across pairs is a cleaner generalization test than tuning pooled across them.
- **H2 (PCD vs normalized-WS equivalence if active rate ≥95%):** **DISAGREE with implication/equivalence test as causal mechanism test.** An inactive PCD step equals the normalized primary before later optimizer state, while an active PCD can still numerically resemble a fixed λ sum. 95% inactive steps do not imply model-level equivalence; and a nonsignificant difference is not equivalence. Predefine paired effect CI and a justified δ. Compare exact per-step direction distributions / parameter updates too.
- **H3 (PCD vs DOT):** exploratory direction is acceptable, but obsolete as the sole optimizer baseline; add DTO-KD if executable under the project constraints, or explain precisely why it cannot be reproduced and compare its published claims cautiously. DOT is not necessarily a universal “SOTA” label.
- **H4 (PCD+LS > LS):** **HYPOTHESIS only**, underpowered as stated with five seeds. Define whether “>” refers to test accuracy, validation-selected checkpoint, or another endpoint. Include a no-PCD and normalized-WS+LS arm; otherwise cannot attribute any additive effect to projection.
- **H5 (KD-primary PCD > CE-primary under 30% corruption):** **HYPOTHESIS only, mechanism interpretation unsupported.** Batch-level corruption ratio and gradient cosine are confounded. Log per-example correctness/confidence and per-example loss/gradient summaries as diagnostics, but do not label the method selective reliability handling.
- **H6 (vs RLD):** target-refinement and gradient-combination methods differ; must compare on identical teacher corruption generation, data split, teacher logits, student schedule and selection budget. RLD's official implementation/protocol/licence is still unresolved. Do not interpret H6 as isolating optimizer choice.
- **H7 (non-inferiority to KD at zero corruption):** **HYPOTHESIS only.** Set δ from practical considerations, and note KD-primary PCD may be inactive or may alter gradients; theory does not imply noninferiority in accuracy.

### Controls, statistics, compute, and failure modes

- **Normalized-WS control is essential but insufficiently defined.** Specify normalization state, bias correction, per-loss EMA initialization/update, whether λ multiplies raw or normalized gradients, final rescale, optimizer state reset, batch reductions, weight decay and LR. For K=3, fit a joint two-weight control, not one scalar. Include PCD's mean realized coefficient as one point, but do not let the validation-derived coefficient consume an extra uncounted tuning decision.
- **Equal tuning budget is not achieved by saying “four points.”** Arm 8 only lists CAGrad values and bundles PCGrad/MGDA; each method needs specified grid/algorithm parameters. DKD tunes β but leaves α/defaults and other values implicit; RLD has its own knobs. Count validation evaluations across all dimensions, fixes and manual iterations; use a fixed compute/selection budget, and clearly separate literature defaults from tuned arms. One-seed selection then five test seeds remains optimistically selected; use a validation-only selection protocol and never test-select.
- **Five seeds are weak for inferential claims.** Bootstrap CIs over five seed pairs are coarse and do not provide robust population uncertainty; TOST with n=5 is especially low power. State seeds as independent training replicates, show all results, use paired seed differences only when seeds genuinely match, and consider more seeds for the primary comparison or reframe as exploratory. Correct Holm families only after defining the family of confirmatory endpoints.
- **Compute arithmetic is internally unclear.** The plan says 10 final arms × 5 seeds per pair, arm 8 counts as three methods, around 240 runs total, while “selection ≈22 per pair.” At 1–2.2 h/run, 240 runs implies roughly 240–528 GPU-h before selection overhead; 350–450 may be plausible but needs row-by-row arithmetic. I1-B's ~130 plus I4's ~160 gives ~290 plus the pilot, hence ~300 is consistent only as a coarse estimate. I4's 7×3×5=105 final runs (not 120) plus selection may approach estimate depending on timings. A measured pilot is mandatory. A single pilot configuration chosen before benchmarking should not be treated as a substitute for an independent estimate across different arms.
- **Major failure modes:** PCD's constraints may not be feasible at K=3; fallback silently reverts to primary; batch-mean cosine masks per-example conflicts; EMA state and adaptive optimizer state confound a one-step guarantee; the primary may ascend; DKD TCKD/NCKD may be coupled; and single validation split/model-selection may favor one arm. Log infeasible/fallback rate, raw and normalized gradients, actual optimizer displacement alignment, corruption fraction and per-example teacher correctness. Freeze data, checkpoints, source commits and train/test separation.
- **Scope:** 10 arms × multiple pairs × five seeds plus seven I4 arms and three corruption levels is not “one standard benchmark” in the practical sense; it is many hundreds of runs and several custom implementations. The assignment fit does not by itself warrant this scope.

## Minimum fixes required before proceeding

1. Recast the novelty claim around **fixed-priority progress constraints / PCD-specific behavior**; add DTO-KD as the direct comparator or document an evidence-based inability to reproduce it. Demote “DOT is optimizer-side SOTA.”
2. Make I1 the sole confirmatory study, starting with P1 and a predeclared small arm set: KD, DOT, DTO-KD (if feasible), PCD, tuned normalized-WS, and one loss-side plug-in (DKD or LS). Treat K=3 and additional pairs as exploratory/extension.
3. Rewrite I4 language: gradient conflict is batch parameter-space alignment, not teacher-label contradiction or per-example trust. Specify corrupted-teacher generation and keep target-repair (RLD) comparison identical; present I4 as a follow-up, not a promised companion contribution.
4. Define the normalized controls and equal tuning budget in executable terms; pin all source commits/configs, verify α/defaults and the P3 LR from mdistiller itself, and reconcile run-count/GPU-hour arithmetic.
5. Replace statistical claims that five seeds can establish equivalence/noninferiority with a justified design (more seeds or explicitly exploratory reporting), predefine primary endpoints, margins and correction families, and test exact optimization behavior as well as accuracy.
6. Lower the scope and GPU estimate to the available semester/budget, then obtain the required approval before downloads or runs.
